This script fits and saves brms models predicting size for each species separately.

The core model is: log(Size) ~ YearRescale + Temp + Latitude + Sex. Alternative models that include a ‘spatial random effect’ by fitting a Gaussian Point Processes to capture additional autocorrelation in sizes explainable by the proximity of locations are also tested.

Towards the end of this the improvement in fit that the spatial-autocorrelation term bring is assessed and the fitted coefficients with and without compared .

Models testing fitting distinct responses to the predictors for each sex afre fit in a seperate document (StatsFitting5)

brms model objects are saved in folders, and summaries of the posteriors are saved as .csvs for further use.

Spatial aggregation and rescaling of lat longs.

In the NHM dataset, lats and longs are given to implausibly precise 4dp precision (approx 10m), presumably due to some transformation along the way. Because fitting time scales with the cube of the number of distinct locations, it is worth just checking if moderate spatial aggregation of very nearby points impacts the number of distinct collection locations.

Fixing Glanville Fritillary Convergence issue

From inspection, clearly problem of GPP sd parameter getting stuck far too high on one of the chains. Dropping dodgy first chain (still leaving three that mixed well) is a neater solution than fiddling with priors, or just rerunning the whole analysis and hoping.

 load(file='../NHM_modelfitsSpatial/ Sp.Melitaea cinxia')

 MODEL_FIT
 mcmc_plot(MODEL_FIT, type= 'trace' ) 

new_fit_wo_stuckchain<- MODEL_FIT

  sim = new_fit_wo_stuckchain$fit@sim
  sim$samples <- sim$samples[-1]
  sim$chains = sim$chains - 1
  sim$warmup2 = sim$warmup2[-1]
  new_fit_wo_stuckchain$fit@sim = sim

  ## Good fit now:
 new_fit_wo_stuckchain
  mcmc_plot(new_fit_wo_stuckchain, type= 'trace' ) 

  MODEL_FIT<- new_fit_wo_stuckchain
save(MODEL_FIT, file='../NHM_modelfitsSpatial/ Sp.Melitaea cinxia')
